**Status:** Phase 1 source-and-licensing register

## 2. Data coverage and project decisions

- **Historical replay window:** 2021-09-30 through 2022-09-29, aligned to the existing `stock_tweets` dataset and historical prices.
- **Recent-news coverage:** GDELT and NewsAPI are agreed sources for recent events/news. Their roles are not being reconsidered in this phase; adapter implementation belongs to the ingestion phase.
- **Existing recent-news file:** `news_sentiment_raw.csv` is retained as a supplementary broad news/market-context dataset. It has no reliable company linkage and must not be represented as a company-level historical news archive.
- **Historical company-news gap:** no adopted, verified historical company-level news source currently fills this gap. FNSPID is deferred due to incomplete accessible coverage and unresolved terms/size. This is a documented limitation, not a reason to delay the current Phase 1 price validation.
- **Ticker scope:** the 16 configured Module A stocks are the stock-specific universe; SPY is the benchmark. Market/event-level records remain valid inputs to the broader risk engine and Module B even when no stock can be reliably linked.
- **Excluded source:** `tweets_returns/` remains out of the pipeline because its date/field alignment is unreliable. Keep the local copy for reference; do not commit it.

## 3. Safe public-repository policy

1. Do not commit `.env`, API keys, credentials, full raw third-party datasets, article text dumps, or large generated market-price files unless explicit rights permit it.
2. Commit code, schemas, configuration, tests, documentation, and small project-authored synthetic fixtures.
3. Commit `.env.example` with blank placeholders only.
4. For each API-ingested item, preserve source and URL metadata where allowed; do not treat a URL as permission to copy or republish article text.
5. If a dataset license is not clear, record it as **unverified** and keep its raw data out of the public repository. Do not claim the licensing review is legally complete merely because a source is publicly downloadable.
6. Recheck source terms before any public hosted deployment or commercial use; terms and pricing can change.
